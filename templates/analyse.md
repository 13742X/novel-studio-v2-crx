# MANUSCRIPT & STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS

Analyse the provided manuscript material as a whole novel. Evaluate:
1. Three-act pacing, turning points and chapter weight.
2. Alignment with genre, strapline and stated premise.
3. Character arcs, agency and relational payoff.
4. Thematic coherence and motif consistency.
5. Prose consistency across chapters (identifying where voice shifts or exposition creeps in).
6. Top 5 highest-leverage revisions for the next draft.
