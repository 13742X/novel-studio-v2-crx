# CHAPTER & SCENE PLANNING

Plan Chapter [NUMBER] ("[TITLE]", Act [ACT]) in detail before prose drafting.

Do not write finished prose yet. Instead produce:
1. Chapter objective & emotional arc (starting state -> turning point -> ending state).
2. Scene-by-scene breakdown (3-5 scenes), each specifying:
   - Scene title
   - POV, characters present, place, time
   - Goal, conflict/obstacle, outcome, and subtext
3. Continuity hooks (facts from previous chapters to honour; details to plant for later).
4. Risks to avoid (cliches, pacing sag, exposition traps).
