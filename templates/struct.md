# Narrative Structure

## Act 1: Establishment (about 20,000 words, chapters 1-7)
Introduce characters, their world, conflicts and the inciting incident.

## Act 2: Confrontation (about 40,000 words, chapters 8-21)
Complications, character development, reversals, revelations and escalating conflict.

## Act 3: Resolution (about 20,000 words, chapters 22-28)
Climax, consequences, resolution and final character arcs.

## For every chapter
What happens? Who is involved? What does the protagonist want? What gets in their way? What changes? What does the reader discover?
