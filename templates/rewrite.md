# PASSAGE REWRITE

Rewrite the supplied passage in the author's voice from the Voice & Style Bible. Keep established events, facts, POV ([POV]) and tense ([PAST/PRESENT]) intact.

Provide three distinct versions:
1. Tighter and more economical.
2. More concrete, observational and sensory.
3. More restrained, relying on subtext rather than explicit emotion.

Do not add exposition or invent major plot developments.
