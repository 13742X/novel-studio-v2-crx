# NOVEL SKELETON & CHAPTER OUTLINE

Using the Project Overview, Novel Bible, Narrative Structure, Character Bible, Location & World Bible and Author & AI Notes, create or refine a chapter-by-chapter skeleton for the novel.

For each chapter provide:
- Chapter number & working title | Act (1, 2, or 3)
- POV character, major/minor characters, location, and time
- Chapter summary (what the POV character wants, what opposes them, what changes by the end)
- 3-5 scene beats

After the detailed outline, provide a compact importable list formatted strictly as:
Title | One-sentence summary
(one chapter per line) so the author can bulk-import chapters directly.
