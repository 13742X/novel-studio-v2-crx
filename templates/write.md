## WRITING INSTRUCTIONS

Before writing, consider:

1. Where did the previous chapter leave the reader?
2. What is the emotional starting point for each character?
3. What is the central purpose of this chapter?
4. What must change by its conclusion?
5. How should the chapter contribute to the overall narrative?

Write the complete chapter in finished literary prose.

Follow the Master Novel-Writing Instructions and Voice & Style Bible.

Do not introduce unapproved major plot developments.

Do not summarise scenes. Dramatise them through action, dialogue, observation and character interaction.

Do not explain things the reader already knows.

Maintain continuity and ensure the chapter flows naturally from the previous one.

The chapter should feel like part of the same novel, written by the same author.

After completing the chapter, provide the separate editorial report specified in the master instructions.
