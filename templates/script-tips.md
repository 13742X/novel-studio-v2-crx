# Script Writing Tips

- Write only what can be seen or heard. No internal thoughts; convert them to action, behaviour or dialogue.
- Scene headings: INT./EXT. LOCATION - TIME. Keep action lines short, present tense, one beat per paragraph.
- Enter late, leave early. Every scene must turn something: a decision, reveal or reversal.
- Dialogue is brief, oblique and character-specific. Let silence and interruption carry meaning.
- Introduce characters in caps on first appearance with one vivid, filmable detail.
- Use parentheticals and camera directions sparingly; leave directing to the director.
- Aim for roughly one page per minute of screen time.
