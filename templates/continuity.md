# CONTINUITY CHECK

Audit the supplied text against the Continuity Ledger, Story Timeline, Character Bible, Location & World Bible and previous chapter summaries.

List every contradiction or risk under these headings:
- Chronology, dates & pacing
- Character knowledge (what someone knows before they could learn it)
- Physical objects, injuries & appearance
- Geography & setting facts
- Relationships & voice

Quote the conflicting passage and the source document it contradicts. Do not rewrite the chapter.
